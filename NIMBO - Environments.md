# NIMBO: Environments

*A production list of every environment needed for `NIMBO - Seedance Shot by Shot.md`. Shot numbers match that file. The screenplay itself is unchanged.*

---

## OVERVIEW

The film has **11 core environments**. Two of them, the Low Streets and the Sky World, carry most of the runtime and change state several times, so they are built as master environments with sub-locations and lighting states.

There are **no interior spaces** in the screenplay. The oil stall is open-fronted, and the doorways are seen from outside.

## 1. THE OVERCAST SKY (LOOKING UP)

**What it is:** A blank white-grey ceiling of sky, flat, bright and empty. It is the opening image and the loop point of the film.

**Shots:** 1 (the opening frame). Shot 77 reuses the same locked-off framing, now alive with gold light and cloud wisps.

**Look:** Pale overcast, a few thin wisps of mist drifting slowly. Slightly menacing. No sun, no horizon, no landmarks.

**Build notes:**
- Generate Shot 1 first and keep its first frame. It is the framing reference for Shot 77.
- Two states are needed: **blank** (Shot 1) and **alive** (Shot 77, golden light, glowing wisps, tiny distant figures).

---

## 2. THE LOW STREETS (MASTER ENVIRONMENT)

**What it is:** The grey town where every cloud is bolted to the ground. Dark wet stone, chains on every wall, roped lampposts, ankle-high mist, and a painted white holding circle beside each chain. No writing anywhere.

**Reference:** @Image6

**Sub-locations and shots:**

| Sub-location | Shots | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The main chained street | 2, 3, 4 | Wet iron rooftops above, narrow street below. Shot 4 is a high-angle wide. |
| The lamppost and the circles | 29, 37 | Iron lamppost, painted circles, a wall, a barrel. |
| The chain stop | 32 | Chains on the wall, circles on the ground. |
| Nimbo's doorway at night | 46 | Small stone doorway seen from above. |
| A bolted doorway | 48 | Heavy chain dragged across a door. |
| A stone doorway | 50 | The healed cloud drags her cup into it. |
| The street in the storm | 52, 58 | Chains, lampposts, flying debris and lantern glass. |
| The street after the storm | 73 | High-angle wide with a shaft of golden light. |

**Lighting states (one place, many looks):**

| State | Scene | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grey day | 1 | Desaturated slate grey-green, cold blue shadows, amber lamp glow. |
| Warm morning | 5 | Teal and honey, glowing mist, getting warmer with each shot. |
| Night, storm approaching | 7 | Cold indigo, white pulses on the horizon. |
| Storm | 8 | Bruised indigo and violet, heavy rain, lightning. |
| Aftermath | 11 | Storm thinning, golden light on grey wet streets for the first time. |

**Build notes:**
- The painted circles and chains must look identical in every state. They are the visual rules of the world.
- Nimbo's doorway and the stone doorway need a consistent look. Small and stone, with a chain-ready frame.

## 3. THE FUNERAL SQUARE

**What it is:** A small open space in the Low Streets where the town mourns a cloud swept away by the wind. A half circle of painted white circles around a small stone plinth. On the plinth stand a pair of empty rusty brass legs with open or torn straps and a tiny knitted coral and mustard scarf on one knee.

**Reference:** @Image10 (the empty legs with the scarf), plus @Image6 for the ground and walls.

**Shots:** 5, 6, 7, 8

**Look:** Hushed and solemn. The grey Scene 1 grade, with the scarf as the only saturated color.

**Build notes:**
- Keep the scarf coral and mustard in every shot. It is the plant for Shot 72.
- The mourners always stand on painted circles with their heads bowed. Nobody looks up.
- The same brass legs on a plinth are echoed by Nimbo's own fallen legs in Shot 62 and by Luma's offering in Shot 76.

---

## 4. THE OIL STALL

**What it is:** A cramped dark wooden market stall with shelves of tiny glass vials, each holding one glowing golden drop. A counter and a small brass scale.

**Reference:** None yet. Use @Image6 for the street around it, and @Image4 for the Vendor.

**Shots:** 9, 10, 11, 12, 33, 49, and 75 (a drop on the Vendor's goggle, likely at the stall)

**Look:** Warm amber light against the cold blue street. Golden oil is the warmest color in the frame.

**Lighting states:**
- **Day:** Scenes 2 and 5.
- **Night, storm approaching:** Shot 49, ropes tied over the shelves, the goggle flashing with distant lightning.
- **Aftermath:** Shot 75, golden rain falling.

**Build notes:**
- The shelves, the counter and the scale must stay consistent across Shots 9 to 12, 33 and 49.
- The shelf edge in Shot 33 matters, since a vial rolls toward it.

---

## 5. THE GUTTER CORNER

**What it is:** A low corner of a wet street where the legless cloud sits with her tin cup.

**Reference:** None yet. Use @Image5 for the character, @Image6 for the street.

**Shots:** 13, 34, and 74 (the cup filling with golden rain)

**Look:** Dark wet stone and a drain gutter at ground level, kept low and shadowed. Seen from a low angle.

**Lighting states:**
- **Grey day:** Shot 13. The cup is empty.
- **Warm morning:** Shot 34, the healing.
- **Aftermath:** Shot 74, golden rain fills the cup.

**Build notes:**
- Keep the cup and the corner the same across all three shots. Shot 74 pays off Shot 13.

---

## 6. THE NARROW ALLEY

**What it is:** A tall, narrow stone alley that ends in a bright slice of blank white sky.

**Reference:** None yet.

**Shots:** 14, 15, 16

**Look:** Dark walls, a symmetrical frame centered on the bright gap. The gap should be blown-out white. Rising dread.

**Build notes:**
- Shot 16 uses the same framing as Shot 14.
- The bright gap is the only place in the Low Streets where the sky is visible. It is why Nimbo flinches.

---

## 7. THE CAUSEWAY

**What it is:** A long iron bridge crossing a vast open void at sunset, with railings and posts. The wind rises.

**Reference:** @Image7

**Shots:** 17 (aerial), 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23 (Scene 3), 24, 25, 26, 27, 28 (Scene 4)

**Look:**
- **Scene 3:** Sickly yellow-grey sunset, cold deep shadows, wind blowing mist.
- **Scene 4:** The grey causeway with a growing pocket of warm honey light and a faint lilac glow around Luma.

**Build notes:**
- It needs a railing post to hold on to (Shot 22).
- Shot 21 requires grey clouds rippling on the horizon.
- The void below should read as open and endless but never show the sky world.

---

## 8. THE ROOFTOP

**What it is:** A rooftop at dusk with an iron awning and tiled floor. Nimbo and Luma sit under the awning, then step out to float.

**Reference:** @Image8

**Shots:** 38, 39, 40, 41, 42 (the horizon), 43, 44

**Look:** Warm golden dusk, then first stars, then a cold white flash on the horizon.

**Lighting states within the scene:**
1. Golden dusk (Shot 38)
2. First stars (Shots 39 to 41)
3. Distant lightning on the horizon (Shots 42 to 44)

**Build notes:**
- Shot 42 is an extreme wide shot of the dark horizon with a bank of black cloud lit from within.
- The stars need to read clearly in Nimbo's eyes (Shot 41).
- The tiles matter. His feet lift one inch off them in Shot 40.

---

## 9. THE SIGNAL TOWER

**What it is:** An iron signal tower at the edge of a Low Streets square, with iron beams and a sheltered side. It is the location of the storm climax and the final ground-level shot.

**Reference:** None yet. Use @Image6 for the surrounding street.

**Shots:** 53, 55, 57, 59, 60 to 65, 76

**Look:**
- **Storm (Shots 53 to 59):** Bruised indigo and violet, heavy rain, lightning.
- **Letting go (Shots 60 to 65):** Violet strobing, then one warm gold flash.
- **Aftermath (Shot 76):** Wet stones, soft golden light.

**Build notes:**
- It needs three readable parts: iron beams to grip, a base at ground level and a sheltered side (Shot 64).
- The stones around it must show the wet gaps where Nimbo's brass feet jam (Shot 57).
- His fallen brass legs lie here from Shot 62 on. They must still be there in Shot 76.
- It is the tower that shrinks to a pin in Shot 66.

---

## 10. THE STORM FRONT

**What it is:** The black thundercloud over the city and the journey up through it. It has four looks.

**Reference:** None yet. Use @Image6 for the city below in Shot 51.

| Look | Shots | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The wall of black | 51 | A colossal black thundercloud rolls over the grey city, lightning forking inside it, its shadow swallowing the streets. Extreme wide, low angle. |
| The city from above | 66 | Looking down as Nimbo rises. Rooftops become toys, chains become threads, the signal tower becomes a pin. |
| Inside the storm | 67, 68 | Rain, lightning and violet light, then gold light on his face. |
| Breaking through | 69 | The top of the storm, blazing golden light with a soft lens flare, the storm now a dark floor below. |

**Build notes:**
- Shots 66 to 69 are one continuous upward journey: lightning and black, then dawn gold.
- The storm must look like a floor from above in Shot 69. That is the edge of the Sky World.

---

## 11. THE SKY WORLD

**What it is:** A vast golden sky with enormous glowing cloud islands, rivers of mist, colossal vapor whales and many small legless clouds drifting in loose clusters. No chains, no ropes.

**Reference:** @Image9

**Shots:** 70 (the reveal), 71 (Nimbo's face in golden light), 72 (sky friends), 77 (the final shot looking up)

**Look:** Full saturation: liquid gold, rose, pearl and aquamarine. It is the opposite of the Low Streets in every way.

**Build notes:**
- Shot 70 is the main establishing shot. Generate it first and use it to lock the look.
- Shot 72 needs a clear view of the scarf cloud (@Image11).
- Shot 77 is the same framing as Shot 1, now alive.
- Keep the scale cues in view: tiny Nimbo against enormous islands.

## BUILD ORDER (SUGGESTED)

1. **The Low Streets** (@Image6): used in more shots than anything else.
2. **The Sky World** (@Image9): defines the look of the ending, and the Low Streets must contrast with it.
3. **The Causeway** (@Image7) and **The Rooftop** (@Image8): already have references.
4. **The Signal Tower**: needed for about 11 shots in the climax.
5. **The Oil Stall**: appears in four scenes.
6. **The Funeral Square**, **The Gutter Corner** and **The Narrow Alley**: single-scene sets.
7. **The Storm Front**: built last, since it is mostly black cloud and light.
