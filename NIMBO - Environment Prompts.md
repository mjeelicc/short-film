# NIMBO: Environment Image Prompts

*Text prompts for generating every environment in `NIMBO - Environments.md` as a still image. These stills become the reference sheets (@Image6 to @Image9 and the new ones) that the Seedance shots in `NIMBO - Seedance Shot by Shot.md` attach.*

**Every prompt here describes the place only.** There are no characters, creatures, figures or events in any prompt. Each prompt is a single block you can copy as is. The "Story function" line above each prompt is for you, not for the image model.

---

## HOW TO USE THESE PROMPTS

1. **Copy the Style line first, then the environment prompt, then the Negative line.** Each prompt below is already complete, so the Style line and Negative line are the only things you add.
2. **Generate the master prompt of each environment first.** Use the variants only after you have a master you like, and attach the master image as a reference when generating the variants, so the architecture stays identical and only the light changes.
3. **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 for everything.
4. **Empty frames:** Leave the open center of each image clear. The characters are added later by the video model, so do not ask the image model to fill the space.

**Style line (add to the start of every prompt):**

> Stylized 3D animated short film look, soft rounded shapes, matte felt-like texture, painterly storybook lighting, gentle film grain, 16:9.

**Negative line (add to the end of every prompt):**

> No characters, no people, no creatures, no figures, no silhouettes, no faces, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no signage writing, no watermark, no photorealism, no harsh outlines.

---

## THE WORLD'S DESIGN LANGUAGE

These rules are baked into every prompt. Keep them in mind when you judge the results, because they are what makes the separate images feel like one film.

| Element | The rule |
| --- | --- |
| **The two worlds** | Below is heavy, grey, cold, angular and rigid. Above is warm, golden, round, soft and flowing. Every environment sits somewhere on that line. |
| **Palette, below** | Slate grey-green (#5E6B6A), cold blue shadow (#2B3A4F), pale overcast white-grey (#D9DCDD), with one warm accent: amber lamp glow (#E8A33D). Golden oil is the warmest color in any frame. |
| **Palette, above** | Liquid gold (#F2B544), rose (#E58A9B), pearl (#F4EFE6) and aquamarine (#6FC9C0). Full saturation. |
| **Materials, below** | Wet dark stone, soot-blackened iron, rivets, rusted and polished brass, worn leather, rope, tarred canvas, thick glass. Everything is damp and slightly dented. |
| **Materials, above** | Soft felt-like cloud, glowing mist, vapor, light. Nothing hard, nothing metal, nothing that makes a sound. |
| **Scale** | The whole world is built at a small, toy-like scale. Doors, chains, lampposts and benches are low and chunky, like a model village. The sky and storm are enormous by comparison. |
| **Recurring motifs** | Chains bolted to every wall, a painted white circle on the ground beside each chain, ropes knotted around lampposts, ankle-high mist. All the circles are plain and blank, never with writing. |
| **The sky** | Below, the sky is a blank white-grey lid that nobody looks at. It must never show a sun, a star or a color until the film's final act. |

## 1. THE OVERCAST SKY (LOOKING UP)

**Story function:** The opening image, a sky nobody looks at, and the loop point of the film. The blank version is Shot 1. The alive version is Shot 77. The two must share the same framing so the ending reads as the opening, transformed.

### 1A. Blank sky (Shot 1)

> A view looking straight up into a blank, flat, overcast sky that fills the entire frame. A seamless pale white-grey (#D9DCDD) with barely visible soft variation, a few very thin, faint wisps of mist drifting across it. No sun, no horizon, no buildings, no birds, no landmarks. The light is flat, bright and shadowless, slightly overexposed so the sky feels like a blank lid or an empty page. Cold neutral grading with a hint of blue in the thin wisps and a slightly sickly green-grey cast at the corners. Matte, felt-like cloud texture, soft painterly gradients, gentle film grain. The feeling is empty, bright, oppressive and quietly menacing, a sky that has nothing to say and that everyone below has learned never to look at.

### 1B. Alive sky (Shot 77, same framing)

> The same view looking straight up into the sky, framed identically to a blank overcast sky, but now alive and radiant. The flat white-grey has opened into a luminous dome of liquid gold (#F2B544) at the center, fading to rose (#E58A9B), pearl (#F4EFE6) and soft aquamarine (#6FC9C0) at the edges. Glowing wisps and ribbons of soft felt-like cloud drift across the whole frame, each lit from within and trailing faint light, with warm shafts of sunlight breaking through between them. Full saturation, painterly storybook light, gentle film grain. No ground, no buildings, no horizon. The feeling is release, wonder and quiet joy, as if the same empty page has finally been filled with color.

## 2. THE LOW STREETS (MASTER ENVIRONMENT)

**Story function:** The grey town where every cloud is bolted to the ground. It is the film's dominant world and has five lighting states. The master image defines the architecture. The variants only change the light, weather and time of day.

### 2A. Master: the chained street, grey day (Scenes 1, Shots 2 to 4)

> A narrow canyon-like street of the Low Streets, seen from a slightly raised wide angle at a toy-like scale. Tall, soot-dark walls of rough, wet, hewn stone lean slightly inward on both sides, with small deep-set windows covered by iron shutters and rusted bars. The rooftops are flat riveted iron sheets, streaked with rain and rust, with short crooked chimney pipes. The street is paved with dark wet cobblestones in a staggered pattern, holding shallow puddles that reflect warm amber lamp glow. Thick black iron chains hang in heavy loops along every wall, anchored to oversized bolt rings, and beside the base of each chain a plain worn white circle is painted on the stones, about as wide as a barrel, blank with no writing. Cast-iron lampposts stand at intervals, each with thick rope knotted around its base and waist and a small glass lantern glowing amber. Ankle-high mist, cold and pale blue, pools across the ground. High above, a narrow slit of blank white-grey overcast sky is visible between the rooftops. Grading: desaturated slate grey-green (#5E6B6A) with cold blue shadows (#2B3A4F), soft overcast light from above, and amber lantern glow (#E8A33D) as the only warm color. Matte stone, damp iron, painterly textures, gentle film grain. The feeling is weary, cautious and heavy, a place built entirely to hold on and never let go.

### 2B. Variant: warm morning (Scene 5)

> The same narrow chained street of the Low Streets, with identical architecture, chains, painted white circles, roped lampposts and cobblestones, now in soft warm morning light. The dark walls are touched by low honey-gold sunlight raking from one end of the street, the wet stones glisten and the mist glows pale gold and teal. Grading: teal (#5FA8A0) and honey (#E9B866), soft warm morning light, glowing mist, shadows that are gentle blue-teal instead of cold. The amber lanterns are still lit but no longer dominant. The sky slit above is a pale warm cream, not yet colored. The feeling is gentle, hopeful and slightly playful, as if the street is thawing for the first time.

### 2C. Variant: night, the storm approaching (Scene 7)

> The same narrow chained street at night, identical architecture and layout, in cold indigo darkness. The sky slit above is deep blue-black with a faint pale pulse of white light at its edge, as if a distant storm is flickering beyond the rooftops. The walls are nearly black, the wet cobblestones hold thin cold reflections, the chains hang taut and still, and the painted white circles glow faintly in the dark like pale moons on the ground. The lanterns on the roped lampposts burn small and amber, dimmer and fewer than by day. A heavy iron chain is visible across one doorway. Grading: cold indigo (#1C2340) with white pulses and a single amber glow (#E8A33D). Heavy atmosphere, thick low mist. The feeling is dread and held breath, a town locking its doors.

### 2D. Variant: the storm (Scene 8)

> The same chained street in the middle of a violent storm, identical architecture. Heavy rain falls in diagonal sheets and whips across the cobblestones, splashing off every surface. The chains sway taut on their bolt rings, the lanterns have burst or gone dark, and loose debris and shards of lantern glass hang suspended mid-air in the wind. The sky above the rooftops is a boiling mass of black and violet cloud torn by branching white lightning that lights the wet stone in hard white flashes. Grading: bruised indigo and violet (#2E2250), black clouds, white lightning flashes, cold wet highlights, deep crushed shadows. Chaotic but readable, with strong silhouettes of the architecture. The feeling is overwhelming, hostile and terrifying.

### 2E. Variant: the aftermath (Scene 11, Shot 73)

> The same chained street from a high wide angle, in the minutes after the storm has broken. The black cloud is tearing apart overhead, and a single broad shaft of golden light falls through it onto the grey wet rooftops and streets. Soft, glittering golden raindrops, each lit like a tiny lantern, fall through the light. The cobblestones shine like wet gold and the puddles reflect the glowing sky. The chains and the painted white circles are still there, but the dark walls are softened by warm light for the first time. Grading: storm-thinning grey and indigo giving way to liquid gold (#F2B544) and rose, with the street still mostly slate grey so the gold feels earned. The feeling is relief, grace and the first breath of hope.

### 2F. Set piece: Nimbo's doorway (Shot 46, night; also used for Shot 47)

> A small, chunky, low stone doorway set into a dark wet stone wall in the Low Streets at night, a toy-scale arched frame with a heavy iron-studded wooden door standing open onto warm amber lamplight inside. A chain hangs from a bolt ring beside the door and a white painted circle is on the cobblestones at the threshold, blank. A single roped iron lamppost stands just outside. Cold indigo night, ankle-high pale mist, thin cold reflections on the stones. Grading: cold indigo (#1C2340) against the small warm amber doorway glow (#E8A33D). The feeling is a small, orderly refuge in a dark, restless night.

### 2G. Set piece: the stone doorway (Shot 50, storm night)

> A deep stone doorway in a dark wet wall in the Low Streets, with a heavy iron-bound door, an iron chain hanging beside it from a bolt ring and a worn stone step. The doorway is an alcove that offers shelter from the weather. Cold indigo night with pulses of white lightning on the wet stones. Grading: cold indigo (#1C2340) with white flashes. The feeling is a cramped, uncertain shelter.

## 3. THE FUNERAL SQUARE

**Story function:** The memorial space in Shots 5 to 8 where the town's one rule is told without words: being swept away means death, the legs keep you alive and nobody looks up. The scarf is the only saturated color in the film's first minute. The plant for Shot 72.

> A small, quiet, open square at the end of a narrow street in the Low Streets, at a toy-like scale and seen at street level. The ground is wet dark cobblestones. A wide half circle of eight plain white painted circles, blank and worn, curves around the center of the square, facing a low round stone plinth about knee height. On the plinth stand a pair of empty rusty brass piston legs, upright, chunky, with two or three visible joints, riveted knee pistons and pitted green-brown verdigris at the seams. Their worn leather straps hang open, with one strap torn and dangling. A tiny knitted scarf in bold coral red (#E5604D) and mustard yellow (#D9A32E) stripes is draped over one brass knee with its fringe lifting slightly in the air. A thin film of golden oil glistens at the plinth's foot. A single dull iron bell hangs from a short iron bracket on a rope on the nearest wall, beside heavy chains with bolt rings. The surrounding walls are tall, soot-dark and shuttered. Above, a narrow slit of blank white-grey overcast sky. Grading: desaturated slate grey-green (#5E6B6A), cold blue shadows (#2B3A4F), soft overcast light, with the scarf as the only saturated color in the whole image. Ankle-high pale mist. Gentle film grain. The feeling is hushed, solemn, reverent and heartbreaking, a place where the town grieves in silence and never lifts its eyes.

## 4. THE OIL STALL

**Story function:** Oil is the world's currency. The stall is where Nimbo's poverty and the Vendor's stern softness play out (Shots 9 to 12, 33, 49, 75). Golden oil must be the warmest, brightest thing in the frame.

### 4A. Master: day (Scene 2 and Scene 5)

> A cramped, dark, open-fronted wooden market stall wedged between two tall wet stone buildings on a Low Streets lane, at a toy-like scale, seen from the front at eye level. A patched tarred-canvas awning shades the front. A heavy, scarred dark-wood counter runs across the opening, with a small polished brass balance scale at one end. Behind it, rows of narrow wooden shelves are lined with hundreds of tiny thick-glass vials, each holding a single softly glowing golden drop of oil (#F2B544) that lights the shelves from within like a wall of tiny lanterns. Brass hooks, coiled copper pipes and strings of small amber lanterns hang overhead. A small painted golden droplet shape, with no text, marks the stall above the counter. Chains hang on the neighboring walls, and a blank painted white circle sits on the cobblestones in front of the counter. The street outside is cold blue (#2B3A4F) with ankle-high mist. Grading: warm amber lamplight (#E8A33D) against cold blue, the golden oil as the warmest color in the frame, deep brown shadows inside the stall. Rich wood grain, tarred canvas, glass reflections, painterly storybook light. The feeling is precious, fragile and tightly guarded, a tiny warm treasure house in a cold town.

### 4B. Variant: night, storm approaching (Shot 49)

> The same oil stall, with identical architecture, shelves and counter, at night. Thick ropes are tied tightly over the shelves, every vial held down by knotted cord. The awning is lashed with ropes and shudders. The amber lanterns are dim and swinging, the golden vials glow brighter against the dark, and the white pulses of distant lightning catch on the brass scale and glass. Grading: cold indigo (#1C2340) with the gold of the vials and a few flickers of white. The feeling is protective and tense, a treasure being battened down.

### 4C. Variant: aftermath (Shot 75)

> The same oil stall at the end of the storm, identical architecture, the ropes now slack and untied, the awning dripping. Golden raindrops fall in a shaft of soft light onto the counter and the brass scale, which gleams. The vials still glow but the light of the sky now matches them. Grading: soft golden light (#F2B544) breaking over slate grey stone, glistening wet wood and brass. The feeling is stillness, softening and quiet wonder.

## 5. THE GUTTER CORNER

**Story function:** A low, forgotten corner where the town's rules have left someone behind (Shots 13, 34, 74). The empty patch must read as waiting.

### 5A. Master: grey day (Shot 13)

> A low, forgotten corner of a wet street in the Low Streets, seen from a low angle at ground level in a toy-like scale. Two dark stone walls meet at a right angle, stained with damp and rust streaks. At their base runs a narrow stone drain gutter, half full of dark water and a few flecks of rust and grit. A small patch of dry, worn-smooth stone sits tucked into the corner, empty, as if someone has sat there many times. A bent, rusty lamppost leans nearby, its lantern dead, a dangling chain hanging from a bolt ring on the wall behind. Ankle-high mist drifts along the ground and a dull gutter puddle reflects the white-grey sky. Grading: desaturated slate grey-green (#5E6B6A), cold blue shadows (#2B3A4F), flat overcast light, no warm colors anywhere except a faint amber hint far down the street. The feeling is cold, ignored, quiet and sad, a corner the town walks past without looking.

### 5B. Variant: warm morning (Shot 34)

> The same gutter corner, with identical walls, drain and lamppost, in soft warm morning light. A low honey-gold sun reaches into the corner and warms the dry stone patch. The gutter water glows gold and teal, the mist glows, and the dead lantern is touched with light. Grading: teal (#5FA8A0) and honey (#E9B866). The feeling is tenderness, the first warmth the corner has ever felt.

### 5C. Variant: aftermath (Shot 74)

> The same gutter corner after the storm, identical architecture, with soft golden rain falling in a single shaft of light into the corner. Tiny glittering golden drops fall onto the dry stone patch and the gutter, which sparkles. The walls are still dark, but the corner glows. Grading: slate grey stone with liquid gold (#F2B544) light and rose highlights. The feeling is grace and being seen at last.

## 6. THE NARROW ALLEY

**Story function:** The one place in the Low Streets where the sky shows through, and the reason Nimbo flinches (Shots 14 to 16). It must feel like a throat opening onto something huge.

> A very narrow, tall stone alley seen in perfect symmetrical one-point perspective from a low wide angle at a toy-like scale. Two towering walls of dark wet hewn stone rise on either side and converge toward the far end, streaked with rust and damp, hung with thick black iron chains on bolt rings and a few rusted pipes. The alley floor is wet dark cobblestones with a thin, shallow trough of water running down the middle, reflecting the far end. At the far end, the walls open into a single bright, blown-out slice of blank white sky, a tall vertical gap of pure overexposed white-grey (#F4F4F2) with no detail, no clouds, no horizon. The glow spills down the walls and across the wet stones, washing out the end of the alley. The foreground is dim and cold, with pale mist at ground level. Grading: amber lamplight (#E8A33D) in the near shadows against cold blue (#2B3A4F), and the overexposed white sky as the brightest and most frightening element. Painterly, heavy contrast, gentle film grain. The feeling is rising dread, a bright threat at the end of a dark tunnel.

## 7. THE CAUSEWAY

**Story function:** The open bridge where the inciting incident happens (Shots 17 to 23) and where Luma and Nimbo meet (Shots 24 to 28). It is the first place with nothing to hold onto except a railing, and the void below must feel endless.

### 7A. Master: sickly sunset (Scene 3)

> A very long iron causeway crossing a vast open void at sunset, seen from a raised wide angle at a toy-like scale. The bridge is a heavy riveted iron truss, with thick lattice girders, oversized rivet heads, a plank-and-iron-plate deck worn smooth and dented, and tall iron railing posts at regular intervals with cross-braced rails. Thin chains hang from the railing posts. Wisps of mist blow sideways through the railings and across the deck. Below and on both sides there is only a deep, endless void of dark violet-black (#1F1B33) with no visible ground and no landscape. The far end of the bridge fades into pale haze. The sky is a sickly yellow-grey sunset (#B9B27A to #8C8E7A) with a low bruised horizon and a few faint grey cloud ripples. Grading: sickly yellow-grey, cold deep shadows (#2B3A4F), wind-blown mist, the light low and weak. Heavy iron, damp rivets, painterly storybook light, gentle film grain. The feeling is exposure, dread and vertigo, a narrow road with nothing around it.

### 7B. Variant: a pocket of warm light (Scene 4)

> The same iron causeway, with identical truss, rail posts and deck, in the same sickly yellow-grey sunset, but now a soft pocket of warm honey light (#E9B866) glows at the center of the bridge with a faint lilac shimmer (#B9A4D8) at its edge, as if the sunset is beginning to warm. The mist near the center glows softly while the ends of the bridge stay cold and grey. Grading: cold grey with a warm honey pocket and a faint lilac glow. The feeling is the first sign of warmth and safety in a threatening place.

## 8. THE ROOFTOP

**Story function:** The place where friendship becomes love and where the midpoint happens (Shots 38 to 44). It must feel like the first beautiful corner of the grey world, while still being part of it.

### 8A. Master: golden dusk (Shot 38)

> A flat rooftop high above the Low Streets at golden dusk, at a toy-like scale, seen from a wide angle. The floor is dark slate roof tiles, overlapping and slightly wavy, with a low stone parapet around the edge. Half the rooftop is sheltered by a sturdy iron awning on slender riveted iron posts, with a patched canvas canopy and a few rope tie-downs. A few short crooked chimney pots stand beside it. Beyond the parapet, the town's rooftops, chimney pipes and chains spread out in layers of cool grey, and at the horizon the sky glows warm gold and soft rose (#F2B544 and #E58A9B) behind a thin band of low cloud. Warm low light rakes across the tiles and the awning, with long soft shadows. Grading: warm golden dusk, grey rooftops still cool, honey light on the wet tiles, gentle film grain. The feeling is the first calm in the world, intimate, romantic and just out of reach of the street below.

### 8B. Variant: first stars (Shots 39 to 41)

> The same rooftop, with identical tiles, awning, parapet and chimney pots, as the last light of dusk fades. The sky above is a deep gradient from rose and amber at the horizon up through violet to dark blue, with the first soft stars appearing, small and clear. The tiles hold a last warm glow and the iron awning is silhouetted. Grading: golden dusk fading to deep violet-blue (#3B3566) with warm star points. The feeling is awe, wonder and a first glimpse of something bigger than the town.

### 8C. Variant: the horizon flickers (Shots 42 to 44)

> The same rooftop at night, with identical architecture. The sky is deep indigo and full of stars at the top, but along the far horizon a distant bank of black storm cloud rises, and deep inside it a flicker of cold white lightning lights it from within, silhouetting the town's distant rooftops. The rooftop tiles are cold and dark, with only a faint pulse of white across them. Grading: cold white flash (#E8EEF5) on the horizon, deep indigo (#1C2340) elsewhere, a few warm star points. The feeling is a beautiful night with a threat underneath it.

## 9. THE SIGNAL TOWER

**Story function:** The climax location (Shots 53 to 65) and the final ground-level shot (Shot 76). It must hold the rope, the beam, the stones where feet jam and the sheltered side where Luma is pushed.

### 9A. Master: the tower in the storm

> A tall iron signal tower standing at the edge of a small cobbled square in the Low Streets, seen from a low wide angle at a toy-like scale. A massive riveted iron lattice frame, black and glistening with rain, rises from a heavy stone base with huge iron beams, diagonal cross-braces and thick bolts. High above, a large brass horn-shaped siren and a dead lantern hang at the top. At the base, one side of the tower is open and exposed with thick horizontal iron beams within easy reach, and the other side is a sheltered lee tucked behind a thick stone buttress, a narrow pocket protected from the wind. The cobblestones are old, wet and uneven, with deep dark cracks and gaps between the stones. Chains hang from the buttress, and ropes are knotted around iron rings at the base. Heavy rain falls in diagonal sheets, mist streams sideways, and the sky above is boiling black and violet with branching white lightning that lights the tower in hard flashes. Grading: bruised indigo and violet (#2E2250), black clouds, white lightning (#E8EEF5), cold wet highlights on iron and stone. Painterly, dramatic, with strong readable silhouettes. The feeling is overwhelming, hostile and monumental, the town's strongest structure against the sky.

### 9B. Variant: the letting go (Shots 60 to 65)

> The same signal tower and square, identical architecture, with the storm at its peak. The sky is a strobing violet (#5B3A8F) with sharp pulses of white light that flatten the scene, and the rain streaks in long diagonal lines. One thin, warm gold glow (#F2B544) appears for an instant at the center of the square, lighting the wet cobblestones and the base of the tower like a single candle. The sheltered lee behind the buttress stays dark. The feeling is the held breath at the edge of a decision.

### 9C. Variant: the aftermath (Shot 76)

> The same signal tower and square after the storm, identical architecture. The black cloud has thinned to broken grey and violet, with a soft shaft of golden light (#F2B544) falling on the tower's base. The iron is dripping, the cobblestones are shining wet, the puddles reflect the golden sky, and glittering golden raindrops fall softly. The cracks between the stones hold small streams of water. The sheltered lee behind the buttress is warm and lit. Grading: bruised violet fading to liquid gold and rose, the tower still dark and heavy. The feeling is exhaustion, relief and the first sense of release.

## 10. THE STORM FRONT

**Story function:** The storm that tears the story open and the passage up through it. It has four distinct looks for four shots or groups of shots.

### 10A. The wall of black (Shot 51)

> An extreme wide low-angle view of a colossal black thundercloud rolling over a grey city, towering across the whole sky like a mountain range of storm. Deep within the cloud, branching white lightning forks and lights its internal chambers with cold white-violet light. Its vast shadow swallows the streets below, and the town's small flat iron rooftops, crooked chimney pipes and thin chains are tiny and grey at the bottom of the frame, already half in darkness. A faint pale line of daylight remains at the very bottom horizon. Grading: black (#0E0E18), bruised indigo and violet (#2E2250), cold white lightning (#E8EEF5), the city a flat slate grey (#5E6B6A). Painterly, enormous scale, gentle film grain. The feeling is terrifying scale, as if the sky itself is falling on the town.

### 10B. The city from above (Shot 66)

> An aerial view looking straight down from high above through a storm. Far below, the Low Streets are shrinking to a toy model, rooftops like small grey tiles, chains thin as threads, lampposts like pins and the iron signal tower a tiny pin at the center. The ground is wrapped in a layer of black and violet cloud with rain streaks and flashes of white lightning lighting it from within. Streaks of rain pass in the foreground. At the edges of the frame a faint gold glow is beginning to appear. Grading: black and violet (#2E2250) at the bottom, the city a cold slate grey, the first hint of dawn gold (#F2B544) at the top edges. The feeling is vertigo and distance, the whole world made small.

### 10C. Inside the storm (Shots 67 and 68)

> A view from inside the storm. Thick turbulent clouds of black, violet and deep indigo swirl in every direction, with long streaks of rain, flashes of branching white lightning and rolling soft walls of mist. There is no ground and no horizon. In the center of the frame the dark begins to thin, and a warm gold glow (#F2B544) bleeds into the violet from above, brightening the churning clouds with rose and amber edges. Grading: violet and lightning (#2E2250 and #E8EEF5) turning to gold. The feeling is chaos transforming into light.

### 10D. Breaking through (Shot 69)

> A low-angle view looking up as the top of a storm breaks open. Below, the black and violet storm stretches out like a dark rolling floor, its churning upper surface lit with rim light. Above, a vast sky of blazing liquid gold (#F2B544) floods the frame with a soft lens flare, shafts of light and a few glowing wisps of cloud, fading up into rose (#E58A9B) and pearl (#F4EFE6). The contrast between the dark floor and the golden sky is total. Grading: black-violet below, full gold and rose above, full saturation. The feeling is sudden, overwhelming awe, like coming up out of deep water into the sun.

## 11. THE SKY WORLD

**Story function:** The world Nimbo never knew existed (Shots 70 to 72) and the final image of the film. It must be the exact opposite of the Low Streets in every way: round, warm, flowing, weightless and generous.

### 11A. Master: the reveal (Shot 70)

> A vast golden sky world, seen from a high wide angle, with no ground anywhere. Enormous glowing cloud islands drift in slow orbits at different depths, each one a huge rounded mass of soft felt-like cloud with scalloped edges, lit from within in liquid gold (#F2B544) and rose (#E58A9B), their undersides shaded in soft pearl (#F4EFE6) and aquamarine (#6FC9C0). Between them, rivers of glowing mist pour in wide ribbons and slow waterfall curves from island to island. In the far distance, colossal whale-shaped formations of vapor drift through the haze as soft, translucent silhouettes made entirely of mist. Small loose wisps of glowing cloud trail light across the middle distance. Shafts of warm sunlight break through between the islands and create deep atmospheric perspective, with layers fading from saturated gold in the foreground to pale pearl at the horizon. There are no chains, no ropes, no metal, no buildings and no hard edges anywhere. Grading: full saturation, liquid gold, rose, pearl and aquamarine, soft painterly storybook light, gentle film grain. The feeling is breathtaking, weightless, generous and joyful, a world that has been waiting just above the grey one.

### 11B. Variant: the intimate sky (Shots 71 and 72)

> The same sky world, with identical cloud islands and mist rivers, seen at a closer, more intimate scale. The soft felt-like cloud is huge and rounded in the foreground, with glowing scalloped edges, and warm golden light fills the air with floating dust-like sparkles and drifting petals of mist. Far behind, the islands are soft and hazy. The light is slightly softer and rosier, with a gentle golden glow on every surface. Grading: warm gold (#F2B544), rose (#E58A9B) and pearl (#F4EFE6). The feeling is tenderness, play and welcome.

### 11C. Variant: the horizon (final image, if the film ends on a wide aerial)

> The same sky world seen from a wide aerial angle with a distant glowing horizon, where a great golden sun softens into rose and pearl haze. Cloud islands of every size drift at every distance, from a tiny puff no bigger than a button in the foreground to colossal islands near the horizon, and ribbons of mist wind between them toward the light. Long soft shafts of sunlight fan out across the whole sky. Grading: full saturation, liquid gold fading to rose and pearl, with aquamarine in the shadows. The feeling is lightness, joy and the open endlessness of a world with nowhere to be.
